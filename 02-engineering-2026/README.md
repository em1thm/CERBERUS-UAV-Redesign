
# CERBERUS — Engineering Redesign (2026)

## Project Objective

To develop and evaluate a technically feasible search-and-rescue UAV concept based on the original CERBERUS project submitted to NASA Space Apps Challenge in 2021.

## Engineering Workstreams

### 1. Mechanical and Aerospace Design
- Multirotor configuration analysis
- Thrust-to-weight calculations
- Propeller and motor selection
- Structural design and mass budgeting
- Parametric 3D modelling in Autodesk Fusion

### 2. Sensing and Mapping
- Ultrasonic sensing feasibility
- Radar-based detection research
- RGB and thermal imaging
- 3D environmental mapping

### 3. Energy and Propulsion
- Battery sizing and flight endurance
- Power consumption modelling
- Hydrogen fuel-cell feasibility

### 4. Autonomous Systems
- Flight control architecture
- Obstacle detection
- Search path planning
- Sensor data processing

## Development Methodology

Each subsystem will be evaluated through:

1. Engineering requirements
2. Mathematical modelling
3. Design alternatives
4. Simulation and testing
5. Results and limitations

## Current Status

Engineering redesign in progress.

The original 2021 concept is preserved separately in the repository.
